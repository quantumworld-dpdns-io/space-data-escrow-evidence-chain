# Retention and Compliance Policy

## Baseline Policy
- Evidence and custody records retained per mission/compliance class.
- Audit trail retention must outlive evidence retention windows.
- Proof bundles for legal/forensic cases preserved under hold.

## Enforcement Controls
1. Scheduled purge jobs in dry-run first.
2. Mandatory audit logging for purge events.
3. Dual-control approval for destructive retention actions.
